# Ethics and Governance Assessment

Write the exact artifact path supplied by the invocation contract.

## Procedure

1. Read the study profile, selected idea, data and methodology contracts, and deployment intent.
2. Determine which requirements are applicable: IRB/ethics review, consent, waiver, privacy, de-identification, data-use agreement, vulnerable populations, biosafety, animal welfare, dual use, security, model harm, and conflicts of interest.
3. Distinguish verified approvals from required-but-unverified approvals. Never invent an approval number, consent process, institution, dataset license, or safety result.
4. Define pre-execution blockers and manuscript disclosures.

- ## Approvals, Consent, and Data Rights
- ## Privacy, Safety, and Dual Use
- ## Equity and Subgroup Risks
