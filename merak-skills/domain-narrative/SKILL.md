# Domain Scientific Narrative

Write `NARRATIVE_REPORT.md` as a manuscript-facing scientific synthesis, not an MERAK execution report.

## Procedure

1. Read the publication profile, selected idea, novelty report, domain methodology, experiment or study plan, result analysis, evidence map, and figure plan.
2. State the scientific problem, why existing work is insufficient, the proposed mechanism or explanatory contribution, the decisive evidence design, actual findings, limitations, and implications in the vocabulary of the discipline.
3. Keep operational statuses, gate IDs, internal claim IDs, retry logs, and platform implementation details out of manuscript prose. They may remain in sidecar audit artifacts.
4. Distinguish observed findings from planned validation and future work. Preserve null, negative, contradictory, and incomplete evidence.
5. Identify the one-sentence contribution, the paper type, the logical section order, the role of each figure/table, and claims that must be narrowed before drafting.
6. Follow the reporting standard and section emphasis declared by `paper/PUBLICATION_STYLE.json`.

Do not create a stronger story than the evidence supports and do not invent results or citations.
